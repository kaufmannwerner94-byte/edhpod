## Pinned post — caption

This is the post that carries the legally required wording. Publish it first, before any content post, and pin it. The image can be the avatar artwork or a simple Ink-background slide with the EDHPod wordmark.

```
Welcome to EDHPod 🃏

One card. One combo. Every day — Commander content for EDH players.

Three formats on rotation:
🟡 Card of the Day — one card worth a slot in your deck
🟢 Budget Combo — two or three cards, full decklist, real prices
🟣 Introduction — what's actually playable from the newest set

How this is made: posts are AI-assisted. Card text, prices and legality come straight from Scryfall data rather than from memory, and every combo is checked line by line before it goes up. If something is still wrong, tell me in the comments — I'd rather fix it than leave it.

Prices are daily estimates in EUR from Cardmarket via Scryfall, and they move.
```

## Standard post caption template

The hashtag block stays identical across posts so it can be appended automatically. Fifteen tags, well inside the limit of 30, and weighted toward Commander rather than Magic generally — a smaller, better-targeted pool is worth more than maximum reach on a new account.

```
{{hook}}

{{two or three sentences of context — the part that isn't already on the slides}}

{{question for the comments}}

Prices are daily estimates (Cardmarket via Scryfall) and move over time.
Unofficial Fan Content. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

#mtg #edh #commander #mtgcommander #magicthegathering #edhdeck #commanderdeck
#mtgdeckbuilding #budgetcommander #mtgcombo #edhrec #mtgfinance #magicdeck
#mtgplayer #commanderformat
```

A short disclaimer line on every post is not strictly required — the policy says to include a note with your Fan Content without specifying placement — but it costs two lines and removes the question entirely.
