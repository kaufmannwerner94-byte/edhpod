Two cards and a couple of mana rocks is infinite mana in any deck with blue in it.

Isochron Scepter has an enters trigger: you may exile an instant with mana value 2 or less out of your hand. It's a trigger, not a replacement effect, so people can respond to it, and if the Scepter dies first the card stays exiled with nothing to show for it. Dramatic Reversal is {1}{U}, mana value 2, and it untaps all nonland permanents you control — the rocks that paid for the activation, and the Scepter itself. So you pay two, tap the Scepter, untap everything, and do it again.

The maths is the whole combo: the loop costs two generic. If your nonland permanents make three or more mana between them, you come out ahead every time around. Sol Ring on its own isn't enough. Sol Ring plus any Signet is.

It's also one of the easiest combos in the format to interact with. Artifact removal, a counterspell on the copy, or anything that taps the Scepter down between loops. People see this one coming.
