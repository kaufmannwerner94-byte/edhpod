Red's answer to literally anything, for three mana, at instant speed.

Chaos Warp has the owner shuffle the permanent into their library, then reveal the top card. If it's a permanent, it comes into play under their control. So it's unconditional removal with a dice roll stapled to it.

What makes the randomness worth it: red has almost nothing else that touches enchantments, so in a lot of decks this is the answer. And because it shuffles rather than destroys, indestructible and regeneration do nothing about it.

One thing worth getting right, because people still say it the old way: Chaos Warp does not tuck a commander. The tuck rule died in 2015. They just put it in the command zone and cast it again for two more. You're buying a turn, not solving the problem.

Most of a Commander deck is permanents, so assume they'll flip into something. The card is at its best pointed at a threat that's worse for you than an average card from their deck would be.
