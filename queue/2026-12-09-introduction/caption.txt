Star Trek's Borg are split across white and black artifact creatures. White hands out keywords: vigilance from Command Subadjunct, double strike from Primary Unimatrix Adjunct. Stack two or three of those and the 1/1 tokens start killing people.

Conquest Mission is the engine piece: a Borg token every time a nontoken artifact lands, and two of them once it has six quest counters.
