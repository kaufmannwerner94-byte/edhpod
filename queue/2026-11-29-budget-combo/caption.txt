Two cards, well under a euro for the pair, and any deck with green and white in it has infinite mana.

Devoted Druid taps for green and can untap itself by putting a -1/-1 counter on it. Vizier of Remedies shaves one counter off every batch of -1/-1 counters headed for your creatures, so the Druid's single counter gets shaved to zero every single time. The untap is free and the Druid never shrinks.

Vizier is a white card, so this is a Selesnya combo, not a mono-green one. Worth knowing before you go looking for it.

The part everyone forgets at the table: the Druid is summoning sick the turn it lands. That is what Lightning Greaves is doing in the list. Without haste you spend a full turn cycle sitting behind a 0/2 that every opponent can see, and somebody will do something about it. Greaves hands out shroud too, so your own protection spells can't target the Druid either.

Once the mana is flowing, Duskwatch Recruiter digs until it finds Walking Ballista, and Ballista turns green mana into damage.
