If you would draw a card, draw two instead. No conditions, no once per turn.

Mr. Spock, Science Officer is four mana for a 2/4 that does nothing on an empty board and doubles everything on a busy one. Every draw from every source counts: your draw step, a cantrip, a Rhystic Study trigger, a wheel, an opponent's Howling Mine.

The interaction worth knowing before you jam it: this is a replacement effect, and two of them don't add, they multiply. Put a second draw doubler alongside it and one draw becomes four. That's how these cards quietly turn a normal turn into a game-ending one.

The rest of the set's blue is quieter, but Captain Kathryn Janeway is doing a lot of work for four mana if you're in green as well.

Is a four-mana draw doubler fast enough for your pod, or does it need to cost two?
