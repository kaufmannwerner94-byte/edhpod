Four mana in white that draws cards while you sit there doing nothing.

White's card draw problem is the oldest complaint in Commander, and Mangara is one of the few answers that doesn't ask you to do anything clever. It sits there with lifelink and cashes in on two very ordinary things: two or more creatures swinging at you or your planeswalkers, and anybody casting a second spell in a turn.

In a four-player pod that second trigger fires far more than you'd guess. Almost everyone has a turn where they cast a mana rock and then a creature, and it counts on your turn too if someone chains two instants.

One thing the slides don't have room for: Mangara changes how people play against you more than it actually draws cards. A table that remembers the trigger exists will send the team somewhere else and hold the second spell for next turn. That's not a consolation prize, that's the card doing its job.
