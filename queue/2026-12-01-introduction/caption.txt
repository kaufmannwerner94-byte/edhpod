Star Trek landed a couple of weeks ago, and the headline mechanic is one most Commander players haven't tried yet.

Station: tap another creature you control and put charge counters equal to its power on that Spacecraft. Hit the number printed on the card and the Spacecraft becomes an artifact creature. Sorcery speed only, and the creature has to be untapped, so you're giving up attacks or blocks to get there. It isn't a tap symbol ability though, so a creature you cast this turn can still station.

U.S.S. Enterprise-D is three mana and colorless, so it's the easiest Spacecraft to drop into a deck you already own. Seven counters turns it into a 4/5 with flying and vigilance — but the reason to run it is the trigger that fires the first time you put counters on it each turn, which exiles the top card of your library and lets you play it. That starts on the very first activation, not at seven.

Deep Space 9 is the expensive one in the set and Borg Cube is the build-around. This is the one you can try for pocket change.
