Play an untapped land and you get two mana out of it. One of them is any color.

Lotus Cobra has a reputation as an expensive card and it stopped deserving it a while ago. Most of the reprints — Iconic Masters, Zendikar Rising, Bloomburrow Commander, Foundations Jumpstart — sit in the $6 / €5 neighbourhood, and the Star Trek Commander one from this month is cheaper still. Only the Secret Lair printing is actually expensive. For a two-drop that ramps and fixes colors at the same time, that's very little money.

One thing the slides don't have room for: the Cobra is a lightning rod. Opponents kill it on sight, which is annoying, but every removal spell aimed at a 2/1 snake is one that isn't aimed at your commander. Some decks run it happily for that reason alone.
