Two red cards and one stack trick, for as many hasty 2/2s as you want.

Cast Twinflame targeting any creature you control and hold priority. Flash in Dualcaster Mage. Dualcaster's trigger copies Twinflame, and you point that copy at Dualcaster itself. The copy resolves, making a hasty Dualcaster token, whose own trigger copies the original Twinflame — which is still sitting on the stack underneath everything, because you never let it resolve.

Every iteration adds another Dualcaster. With Impact Tremors on the battlefield that's as much damage as you'd like to deal.

One thing to plan around: Twinflame exiles the tokens at the beginning of the next end step. This wins the game this turn or it does nothing, so don't start it hoping to untap with the board.
