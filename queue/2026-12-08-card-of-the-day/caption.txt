One mana. Equip for one. Your 1/1 dies and you draw two cards.

Skullclamp is banned in Modern and Legacy and costs about the same as a pack of sleeves. The +1/-1 isn't a drawback, it's the entire engine — anything with one toughness is a "draw two" waiting to be clicked.

It's colorless, so there's no deck in the format that can't play it. If you make tokens at all, it's very likely the best card draw available to you at any price, let alone this one.

The discipline is remembering it's an equipment and not an aura. Equip is an activated ability, so you can re-equip as many times in a turn as you can pay for, any time you could cast a sorcery. On a big token turn one Skullclamp draws you ten cards. Players who treat it as a one-shot leave most of the card on the table.
