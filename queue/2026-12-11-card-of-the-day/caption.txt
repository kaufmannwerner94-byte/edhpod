Three mana, instant speed, exile any nonland permanent.

No restriction on what it hits. Not "nonlegendary", not "with mana value 3 or less". Anything that isn't a land, gone, for three mana and three life.

Exile matters more in Commander than in any other format. Indestructible commanders, creatures with death triggers, recursive threats that come back from the graveyard every turn — a destroy effect just hands them another turn. This doesn't give anything back.

The three life is the honest cost and it's easy to underrate. In a deck already running shocklands, fetches and life-payment tutors, two of these in a game adds up faster than it looks on paper. Worth it almost every time, but worth counting.
