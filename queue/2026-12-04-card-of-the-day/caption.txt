For most of Magic's history black simply could not kill an enchantment. Then they printed this at common.

Two mana, sorcery speed, destroy target creature or enchantment an opponent controls. You lose life equal to its mana value.

It's hard to overstate the hole this filled. Mono-black decks used to need a colourless answer like Nevinyrral's Disk, or a splash, or just the hope that somebody else dealt with it.

The life loss is real but it scales with the problem rather than with your life total. Killing a three-mana enchantment costs three life, which is nothing. Killing a nine-drop is a decision you'll actually have to think about, and that's a fair place for the drawback to live.
